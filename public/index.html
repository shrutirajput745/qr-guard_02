How QR Guide 2 Works

A step-by-step overview of how the security engine processes and analyzes QR codes.

01

1. Scan or Upload QR

Users scan a physical QR code using live webcam video feed or drag-and-drop an image file (PNG, JPG, WEBP).

02

2. Extract URL or Text

The decoding engine parses matrix barcodes and extracts the underlying web link, Wi-Fi config, or text payload.

03

3. Analyze Security Indicators

Evaluates 9 key indicators including HTTP encryption, IP hostnames, high-risk TLDs, `@` tricks, and phishing keywords.

04

4. Calculate Risk Score

Assigns itemized risk points (0–100) based on detected anomalies for full scoring transparency.

05

5. Display Security Result

Presents a clear verdict (Safe, Suspicious, or Dangerous) with explanations and actionable safety advice.

Why QR Security Matters

Understanding Quishing & QR Phishing Risks

QR codes hide raw URLs behind visual matrix patterns. Attackers exploit this opacity—a threat known as "Quishing" (QR Phishing)—to silently redirect users to malicious websites without revealing the destination beforehand.

Fake Credential Harvesting

Phishers replace legitimate QR stickers on posters or menus with links pointing to fake bank or login pages.

Hidden Obfuscation Tricks

Shortened URLs (`bit.ly`) and IP hostnames disguise dangerous server locations from quick glance inspection.

Drive-By Malware Downloads

Malicious QR codes can trigger automatic downloads or exploit unencrypted HTTP connections.
